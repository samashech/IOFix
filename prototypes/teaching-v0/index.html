01 / YOUR REFERENCE

Make your first LED blink

Arduino Uno · External LED · Digital pin 8

Logical circuit · A = anode · K = cathode

Before changing a connection

Disconnect USB power. This lab uses only a USB-powered Uno and a low-voltage LED circuit.

Parts & breadboard guidance
  • Arduino Uno and USB cable
  • LED and 220–330 Ω resistor
  • Breadboard and jumper wires

Each connected strip is one electrical node. Put the LED legs on different nodes. Breadboard rails may be split; verify your board’s layout.

Reference Arduino sketch

Upload this sketch before troubleshooting. It uses D8, not the built-in LED pin.
